About

How I approach a build

I'm a WordPress developer focused on small business and B2B sites — the kind of client who needs a site they can update themselves, without learning code or depending on a developer for every small change.

Every project starts with the same question: what does the owner need to change on their own? That decides what becomes a Custom Post Type, what becomes an ACF field, and what stays fixed in the design.

  • Structure content first — post types and fields — before touching layout
  • Build with Elementor so design stays flexible after launch
  • Keep the client's admin dashboard simple, not overwhelming
  • Test every form and every editable field before calling it done
